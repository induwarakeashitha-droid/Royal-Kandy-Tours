"Immerse yourself in thousands of years of royal history, misty cloud forests, thrilling safari adventures, and serene palm-fringed coastlines — seamlessly guided from start to finish."

Our signature 10-day private itinerary is designed for travelers who want an effortless, all-encompassing journey through Sri Lanka's icon destinations. Travel in comfort with your dedicated English-speaking chauffeur-guide, enjoying a carefully curated, comfortable journey.

Every element is tailored to your pacing: skip standard tourist rushes, enjoy authentic village lunches, take the world-famous Kandy to Ella train ride, experience jeep safaris deep inside Yala, and soak up the heritage charm of Galle Fort.

Detailed Schedule

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Customizable upon request
Day 01

Arrival in Colombo & Transfer to Sigiriya

Sigiriya

Welcome to Sri Lanka! Meet your private chauffeur-guide Nalinda upon arrival at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB). Board your air-conditioned vehicle and journey through lush coconut groves to the heart of the Cultural Triangle in Sigiriya.

Check in to your luxury eco-resort, unwind by the pool, and enjoy a traditional Sri Lankan welcome dinner.

Meals: Dinner
Day 02

Sigiriya Rock Citadel & Village Safari

Sigiriya

Ascend the iconic 5th-century Sigiriya Rock Fortress (Lion Rock) in the cool morning air. Marvel at ancient frescoes, mirror walls, and the summit palace ruins overlooking 360-degree jungle canopy.

In the afternoon, experience an authentic village safari: ride a bullock cart, cross a lotus lake on a wooden catamaran, and participate in a live traditional cooking demonstration with local hosts.

Meals: Breakfast, Village Lunch, Dinner
Day 03

Dambulla Cave Temple & Drive to Kandy

Kandy

Explore the UNESCO-listed Dambulla Cave Temple complex, housing over 150 serene Buddha statues and magnificent ancient ceiling murals carved into solid granite caves.

Continue drive southwards to Kandy, stopping at a spice garden in Matale to learn about Sri Lankan cinnamon, cardamom, and herbal medicine. Evening check-in at a hillside location overlooking Kandy Lake.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Day 04

Kandy Cultural Highlights & Sacred Temple

Kandy

Discover the last royal capital of Sri Lanka. Stroll around Kandy Lake, visit the Royal Botanical Gardens of Peradeniya featuring giant palm avenues and orchid houses.

In the evening, attend the traditional Kandyan Cultural Dance performance followed by a evening ceremony at the sacred Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa).

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Day 05

Scenic Hill Country Train Journey to Ella

Ella

Board the famous mountain blue train from Peradeniya / Nuwara Eliya to Ella. Ride through rolling emerald tea estates, mist-covered valleys, and cascading waterfalls — renowned as one of the world's most scenic train rides.

Your driver will pick you up at Ella station and check into your mountain view suite overlooking Ella Gap.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Day 06

Ella Gap, Nine Arch Bridge & Little Adam's Peak

Ella

Wake up to crisp mountain air. Walk through tea fields to the iconic Nine Arch Bridge in time to watch the train pass across the colonial stone viaduct.

Take an easy afternoon hike up Little Adam's Peak for 360-degree views of Ella Gap, followed by relaxing at a panoramic mountain café or zip-lining experience.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Day 07

Ella to Yala & Private 4x4 Wildlife Safari

Yala

Descend from the central highlands through Ravana Waterfalls toward the southern dry zone. Arrive in Yala by afternoon.

Embark on an exciting 4x4 private jeep safari inside Yala National Park — home to one of the world's highest density of leopards, Asian wild elephants, sloth bears, and crocodiles.

Meals: Breakfast, Safari Snacks, Dinner
Day 08

Coastal Journey to Mirissa & Coconut Tree Hill

Mirissa

Drive along the scenic southern shoreline to the tropical paradise of Mirissa. Check in to your beach resort.

Spend the afternoon relaxing on golden sands, watching stilt fishermen in Weligama, and catching sunset at the famous Coconut Tree Hill vantage point.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Day 09

Mirissa Whale Watching & Historic Galle Fort

Galle

Optional early morning catamaran excursion to spot Blue Whales and playful dolphins off the coast of Mirissa.

Later, explore UNESCO World Heritage Galle Fort. Walk cobblestone streets lined with Dutch-colonial villas, artisan boutiques, lighthouse views, and sunset watchpoints over the Indian Ocean.

Meals: Breakfast, Farewell Dinner
Day 10

Galle to Colombo & Airport Departure

Colombo / CMB Airport

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast at Galle Fort. Drive along the Southern Expressway toward Colombo for a quick panoramic city tour, last-minute souvenir shopping, or tea tasting.

Transfer to Colombo International Airport (CMB) with memories to last a lifetime. Safe travels!

Departure Transfer Included Meals: Breakfast
