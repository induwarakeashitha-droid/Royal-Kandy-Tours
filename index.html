Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking private chauffeur-guided tours across Sri Lanka.

Is it safe and comfortable to hire a private driver in Sri Lanka?

Yes, touring Sri Lanka with a private English-speaking chauffeur guide like Nalinda is the safest, most comfortable, and stress-free way to travel. You have private air-conditioned vehicle transportation, dedicated local navigation expertise, and total flexibility to stop at scenic viewpoints whenever you wish.

What is included in Royal Kandy Tours packages?

Our comprehensive tour packages include your private air-conditioned vehicle (sedan or spacious van), all fuel, expressway toll fees, parking charges, driver-guide accommodation, meals for the driver, airport transfers, and 24/7 personal travel assistance throughout your stay.

Can we customize our Sri Lanka tour itinerary?

100% yes! Every itinerary can be fully customized around your flight schedule, interests, budget, and travel pace. Whether you wish to add whale watching in Mirissa, explore ancient tea factories in Nuwara Eliya, or embark on a Yala safari, we tailor the journey specifically for you.

How do airport pick-ups and drop-offs work?

Nalinda will personally meet you at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) in Colombo holding a personalized name board at the arrival gate. We handle all luggage and transfer you directly to your first hotel in absolute comfort.

What currencies and payment methods are accepted?

We accept multiple currencies including EUR (€), GBP (£), USD ($), CHF, AUD, and LKR. You can pay securely upon arrival in Sri Lanka or via international bank transfer with clear transparent pricing and no hidden fees.

What is the best time of year to visit Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka is a year-round tropical destination! The south and west coasts (Galle, Mirissa, Bentota) and central highlands (Kandy, Ella) are sunniest between November and April. Meanwhile, the cultural triangle and eastern coast beaches peak from May to September.
