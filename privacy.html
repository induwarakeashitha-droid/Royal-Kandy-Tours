Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Royal Kandy Tours respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you send a booking request through our website, you may give us:

  • Your name, email address, phone / WhatsApp number and country of residence.
  • Trip details such as your chosen tour, travel dates, group size, interests, budget and any special requests (for example dietary or mobility needs).
  • How you heard about us and how you prefer to be contacted.

We also receive any information you choose to share when you email, call or message us.

2. How your booking request is sent

Our booking form does not store your details on our website. When you submit it, your details are put into a WhatsApp message that opens on your own device, and they reach us only when you press send. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and plan your itinerary.
  • To arrange and run your tour, including transport, accommodation and activity bookings.
  • To contact you before, during and after your trip about your booking.

4. Sharing your information

We never sell your personal information or share it for marketing. Where needed to deliver your tour, we share only the details required with trusted providers such as hotels, safari operators or ticketing offices.

5. Cookies and third-party services

Our website uses cookies and some third-party services, such as our reviews widget. See our Cookie Policy for details.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your information only for as long as we need it to handle your enquiry, deliver your trip and keep basic business records.

7. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@royalkandytours.com or on WhatsApp at +94 77 026 6988.
