7-Day Highlights Tour

Sigiriya, Kandy & Ella — sample Sri Lanka's greatest ancient heritage sites and breathtaking mountain landscapes in one perfectly paced week.

Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights
Group Style 100% Private
Vehicle Luxury AC Sedan/Van
Meals Included Daily Breakfast & Dinner
Guide Private Driver-Guide
Price From $700 / person

"Short on time but want to experience Sri Lanka's most famous bucket-list wonders? This 7-day private tour captures the essence of the island with absolute comfort."

Designed for travelers with a 1-week holiday window, this tour connects the world-famous Cultural Triangle (Sigiriya Fortress & Dambulla Caves) with the royal kingdom of Kandy and the lush cloud forests of Ella.

You will enjoy private door-to-door transportation, reserved tickets for the famous Kandy-to-Ella blue train, and personal attention from Nalinda, your experienced chauffeur-guide.

Climb ancient 5th-century Sigiriya Lion Rock
Explore UNESCO Dambulla Cave Temples
Visit Kandy's Sacred Temple of the Tooth
World-famous scenic train journey to Ella
Photo stop at Nine Arch Viaduct in Ella
Tea estate tour & tasting in Nuwara Eliya

Tour Route Overview

Colombo Airport Sigiriya Dambulla & Kandy Ella Highlands Nuwara Eliya Colombo Airport
7-Day Itinerary

Day-by-Day Schedule

Customizable
Day 01

Airport Pickup & Drive to Sigiriya

Sigiriya

Meet Nalinda at Colombo Airport. Relax as you are driven into the Cultural Triangle. Settle in and enjoy a peaceful evening amidst tropical greenery.

Meals: Dinner
Day 02

Sigiriya Rock Climb & Dambulla Caves

Sigiriya

Climb Sigiriya Rock Fortress early in the morning to explore King Kashyapa's sky palace. Afternoon visit to the historic Dambulla Golden Cave Temple complex.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Day 03

Matale Spice Garden & Kandy Temple Tour

Kandy

Drive to Kandy via Matale spice gardens. Visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic and watch a vibrant Kandyan cultural drum & dance performance.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Day 04

Kandy to Ella Mountain Train Ride

Ella

Board the scenic blue train to Ella. Wind through tea gardens and misty mountain passes. Arrive in Ella and check into your mountain view room.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Day 05

Nine Arch Bridge & Little Adam's Peak Hike

Ella

Explore the Nine Arch Bridge, hike up Little Adam's Peak for panoramic views of Ella Gap, and stop by Ravana Falls.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Day 06

Nuwara Eliya Tea Estate & Factory Tour

Nuwara Eliya

Visit "Little England" (Nuwara Eliya). Tour a working Ceylon tea factory, sample fresh high-grown tea, and walk around Gregory Lake.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Day 07

Return Transfer to Colombo / Airport

Airport

Scenic morning drive down the hills to Colombo or direct transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport for your departure flight.

Airport Drop-off Included Meals: Breakfast
