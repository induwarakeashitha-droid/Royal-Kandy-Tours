Southern Coast

Galle

"A living museum by the sea — Galle Fort is where centuries of Portuguese, Dutch, and British colonial history meet the warm rhythms of Sri Lankan life."

Galle is a UNESCO World Heritage city that wears its layered history beautifully. Its 17th-century Dutch fort, with walls of coral and granite jutting into the Indian Ocean, encloses a charming labyrinth of cobblestone streets lined with boutique shops, galleries, and cafés. Beyond the fort, pristine beaches and the slow-rolling surf of the southern coast await.

Galle Fort

Stroll along the 17th-century ramparts at sunset as waves crash below — one of the finest colonial forts in Asia.

The Old Lighthouse

Built in 1938 on the site of a Dutch lighthouse, this landmark stands at the tip of the fort offering sweeping ocean views.

What to Experience

  • 01.

    Walk the Fort Ramparts at Sunset

    The most iconic Galle experience — stroll the ancient walls as the sun dips into the Indian Ocean, painting the sky gold.

  • 02.

    Explore the Old Town's Galleries & Boutiques

    The fort's cobblestone streets are home to some of Sri Lanka's finest jewellery, antique, and art shops — perfect for unique souvenirs.

  • 03.

    Day Trip to Mirissa & Whale Watching

    Just 30 km east, Mirissa's beach and whale watching season (November–April) make for an unforgettable day excursion from Galle.
