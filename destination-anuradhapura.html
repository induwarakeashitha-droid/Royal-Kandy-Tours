Cultural Triangle

Anuradhapura

"The first capital of an ancient civilization, Anuradhapura is a sprawling sacred city where colossal white stupas rise above the jungle canopy, unchanged for two millennia."

Anuradhapura is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. For over a thousand years, it was the capital of Sri Lanka and a thriving centre of Theravada Buddhism. Today, its vast sacred precinct is a pilgrimage site for millions, dotted with towering dagobas, ancient ponds, and the world's oldest documented tree — the revered Sri Maha Bodhi.

Ruwanwelisaya Stupa

One of the tallest ancient monuments in the world, this great white dagoba was built by King Dutugemunu over 2,000 years ago.

Sri Maha Bodhi

The sacred fig tree grown from a cutting of the tree under which the Buddha attained enlightenment — over 2,300 years old.

What to Experience

  • 01.

    Explore the Sacred Precinct by Bicycle

    The best way to navigate the vast ancient city — cycle between towering stupas, moonstone-flanked temples, and sacred pools at your own pace.

  • 02.

    Visit Mihintale at Sunrise

    The birthplace of Buddhism in Sri Lanka, just 13 km from Anuradhapura, with a hilltop stupa offering panoramic views over the jungle.

  • 03.

    Attend Evening Puja at Ruwanwelisaya

    Join white-clad pilgrims as drums beat and incense smoke rises around the illuminated stupa — a profoundly moving experience.
