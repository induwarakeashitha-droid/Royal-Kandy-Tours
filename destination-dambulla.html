Cultural Triangle

Dambulla

"Carved into a vast granite outcrop rising above the jungle, the Dambulla Cave Temple is one of the most awe-inspiring sacred spaces in all of Asia."

Dambulla is the gateway to Sri Lanka's Cultural Triangle, anchored by its extraordinary cave temple complex — a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Five interconnected caves contain over 150 Buddha statues and exquisite murals covering 2,100 square metres of ceiling, painted by devotees across 22 centuries. Nearby, the towering Sigiriya Rock awaits just 20 km away, making Dambulla an unmissable base for exploring the ancient heartland.

The Cave Temples

Five caves of reclining and seated Buddhas, some over 15 metres long, surrounded by ancient frescoes of extraordinary colour.

Views from the Rock

Climb 160 metres above the plains for panoramic views stretching across the jungle to Sigiriya Rock on the horizon.

What to Experience

  • 01.

    Explore the Golden Cave Temple

    Wander through five cave chambers filled with 2,300 years of devotion — vivid murals, gilded statues, and the scent of incense in the cool rock air.

  • 02.

    Day Trip to Sigiriya Rock Fortress

    Just 20 km away, the iconic Lion Rock and its ancient palace ruins and frescoes make a perfect companion to Dambulla on the same day.

  • 03.

    Visit Nalanda Gedige

    A unique 8th-century temple that blends Hindu and Buddhist architectural styles, located on an island in a serene reservoir nearby.
