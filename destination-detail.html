Central Highlands

Ella

"A small village nestled in the heart of the central highlands, Ella is a paradise for hikers, nature lovers, and anyone seeking serenity in the clouds."

Ella is one of the most charming destinations in Sri Lanka. It’s a place where you can take it slow, walk through emerald tea plantations, and listen to the sounds of cascading waterfalls. The cool mountain air and the breathtaking views from Ella Gap make it an unforgettable stop on any itinerary.

Ella Gap

Breathtaking views looking out between the mountains towards the southern plains.

The Kandy-Ella Train

Arguably the most beautiful train journey in the world, passing through tea estates and tunnels.

What to Experience

  • 01.

    Hike Little Adam's Peak

    An easy hike for panoramic views of the entire region, best enjoyed at sunrise.

  • 02.

    Visit Ravana Falls

    One of the widest waterfalls in the country, steeped in ancient Hindu mythology.

  • 03.

    Nine Arch Bridge at Noon

    Witness the iconic train pass over the architectural marvel of the colonial era.

Quick Facts

Elevation 1,041 m
Best Time Jan — May
Climate Cool & Misty
