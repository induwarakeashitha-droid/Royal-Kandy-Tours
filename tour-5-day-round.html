5-Days Round Tour

Explore Sigiriya, Polonnaruwa, Minneriya Elephant Safari, sacred Kandy, and Pinnawala in 5 action-packed days with private chauffeur-guide service.

Duration 5 Days / 4 Nights
Group Style 100% Private
Vehicle Luxury AC Vehicle
Meals Included Daily Breakfast & Dinner
Guide Private Driver-Guide
Price From $500 / person

"An ideal 5-day round tour packed with ancient history, wild elephant encounters, royal heritage, and local Sri Lankan warmth."

If you have limited days or are taking a quick getaway to Sri Lanka, this 5-day private tour covers the country's most essential northern & central highlights without long, tedious transit times.

You will climb Sigiriya Rock Citadel, tour ancient Polonnaruwa, witness hundreds of wild elephants at Minneriya National Park, and visit Kandy's famous Temple of the Tooth.

Climb Sigiriya Rock Fortress at sunrise
Tour ancient medieval city of Polonnaruwa
Wild Elephant Safari at Minneriya National Park
Visit Dambulla Golden Cave Temple
Temple of the Tooth & Kandyan Dance Show
Pinnawala Elephant Sanctuary visit

Tour Route Overview

Airport Sigiriya Polonnaruwa & Minneriya Dambulla & Kandy Pinnawala & Airport
5-Day Schedule

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Customizable
Day 01

Airport Pick-up & Transfer to Sigiriya

Sigiriya

Warm welcome by Nalinda at Colombo Airport. Travel directly to Sigiriya in your private air-conditioned vehicle. Evening relaxation.

Meals: Dinner
Day 02

Sigiriya Rock Climb & Ancient Polonnaruwa

Polonnaruwa

Climb the famous Sigiriya Rock Fortress in the morning. Later explore the medieval capital of Polonnaruwa, seeing the colossal Gal Vihara rock-carved Buddha statues.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Day 03

Minneriya Elephant Safari & Dambulla Caves

Minneriya

Visit Dambulla Golden Cave Temple. In the afternoon, jump into a 4x4 open jeep for an unforgettable safari in Minneriya National Park to spot herds of wild elephants gathering by the lake reservoir.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Day 04

Drive to Kandy & Sacred Temple of Tooth

Kandy

Scenic drive to Kandy via Matale spice gardens. Visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, stroll along Kandy Lake, and enjoy an evening Kandyan cultural show.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Day 05

Pinnawala Elephant Sanctuary & Departure

Airport Transfer

Morning tour of Royal Botanical Gardens of Peradeniya and stop at Pinnawala Elephant Sanctuary to watch elephants river bathing. Direct transfer to Colombo Airport for your departure flight.

Airport Drop-off Included Meals: Breakfast
