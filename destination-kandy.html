Cultural Triangle

Kandy

"Sri Lanka's cultural capital, Kandy is a city of sacred temples, royal traditions, and misty green hills — a place where ancient kingdoms still breathe."

Nestled in a valley surrounded by forested hills, Kandy was the last royal capital of the Sinhalese kings. At its heart lies the revered Temple of the Tooth Relic — one of the most sacred Buddhist shrines in the world. Stroll along the serene Kandy Lake, witness the mesmerising Kandyan dance performances, and soak in a city that has earned its place on the UNESCO World Heritage list.

Kandy Lake

A tranquil artificial lake built in 1807 by the last Kandyan king, perfect for an evening stroll.

Pinnawala Elephants

Just an hour from Kandy, the Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage is home to one of the world's largest captive elephant herds.

What to Experience

  • 01.

    Temple of the Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa)

    One of Buddhism's holiest sites, housing the sacred tooth relic of the Buddha. Visit at puja time for a truly spiritual experience.

  • 02.

    Esala Perahera Festival

    One of Asia's grandest festivals — a magnificent procession of elephants, fire dancers, and drummers held each July/August.

  • 03.

    Royal Botanical Gardens, Peradeniya

    One of the finest botanical gardens in Asia, spanning 147 acres with an extraordinary avenue of royal palms.
