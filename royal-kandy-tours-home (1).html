About Us

Based in Kandy, Royal Kandy Tours is a private driver-guide service built around one idea: your trip should feel personal, not packaged. Every itinerary is planned by hand, shaped around what you actually want to see.

From temple visits and hill country drives to wildlife safaris and coastal getaways, we take care of routes, timing, and local knowledge — so you can simply enjoy the island.
